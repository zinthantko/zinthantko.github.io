ZComics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What ZComics is

ZComics is a comic reader app for original Burmese comics by ZinThantKo. It shows a library of comics and lets you read them page by page. The comic library is loaded from a public Google Sheet, and comic files are hosted on Google Drive.

Information we collect

ZComics does not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal information. The app itself does not collect, store, or share your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

Advertising

ZComics shows ads provided by Mondiad. The ad provider may use your device advertising identifier and general technical information (such as device type and approximate region) to deliver and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your Android device settings.

Third-party services

Comic content is delivered through Google Sheets and Google Drive. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply when the app loads their content.

Children

ZComics is intended for a general audience and contains no adult content. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page.
